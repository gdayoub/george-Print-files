# Sculpt V6 - visual references and construction

Consulted on 2026-09-26:

- [Official Pokemon UNITE Gengar](https://unite.pokemon.com/en-gb/pokemon/gengar/) - rounded continuous silhouette, pointed ears, back spikes, angular eyes and broad grin.
- [Official Pokemon Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokedex/gengar) - character reference.
- [Jazwares Gengar figure photographs](https://www.eliveshop.de/pokemon-battle-feature-figur-gengar-11-cm) - short limbs, ear proportions, and clean transitions between forms.

These are visual references. Their images and any third-party character mesh are not included in this release. The new exterior is generated by `CAD/sculpt_character.py`, using a continuous rounded body and smoothly blended ears, limbs and spikes. It replaces the distorted bowl exterior used in earlier revisions. The supplied bowl remains in `Foundations` as an archived project reference.

The eye and grin outlines are Bezier curves cut into the curved exterior as aligned color volumes. The original Puffco base and lid are fitted afterward and retain their exact source geometry under rigid placement. The small underside cup uses a separate thread.

Previews render the exported CAD meshes. They are not image-generation mockups. Black, red and pink are retained for the user's available filament colors.
