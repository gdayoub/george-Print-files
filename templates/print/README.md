## MakerWorld & support

Add a verified profile/model URL only after publication. Until then, say “coming soon” without a fake button. Optional copy: “If you enjoy the design, consider a Boost on MakerWorld when available, share a make, or leave constructive feedback. Support is optional and appreciated.” Link to the repository’s support guide.

## Suggested folder layout

```text
README.md
project.json
CREDITS.md
images/             Cover render, photos and labelled references
files/stl/          Recommended geometry
files/3mf/          Verified slicer projects, when available
source/             Editable design and regeneration instructions
docs/               Printing, assembly and version history
validation/         Digital checks and documented physical results
downloads/          A clearly versioned, self-contained package
```

Use charcoal backgrounds and red accents for new cover artwork. Lead with an image of the actual geometry or print, make the download easy to find, and keep status labels explicit. Add the project to both the root gallery and `catalog.json`.
