# Printing and assembly

[← Project](../README.md)

## P1S starting settings

Use your actual nozzle, filament, and build plate profiles. These settings assume a 0.4 mm nozzle and PLA.

| Setting | Purple body | Red eyes | White grin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Layer height | 0.20 mm | 0.16 mm | 0.16 mm |
| Walls | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Infill | 12% gyroid | 12% gyroid | 12% gyroid |
| Supports | Tree Auto; review arms and overhangs | Off; flat back down | Off; flat back down |
| Prime tower | Off | Off | Off |

Slice the three colors as separate jobs. Combining them on a print-by-layer plate can reintroduce filament changes. Print upright/flat as supplied, at 100% scale. Obtain time and filament estimates from your slicer.

## Assembly

1. Cool the parts and remove supports or brim. Keep insert backs flat.
2. Dry-fit the mirrored eyes and single grin. These are glue-in parts, not snap fits. Lightly sand a tight edge if necessary.
3. Apply a small amount of plastic-compatible glue in each recess. Seat the sculpted faces outward and follow the adhesive's curing instructions.
4. Add pupils, tooth separations, and outlines with a fine black acrylic paint pen.
5. After the adhesive and paint cure, check the fit with your can.

The grin is one connected insert, so individual teeth do not need assembly.
