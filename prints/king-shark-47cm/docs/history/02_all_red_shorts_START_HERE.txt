KING SHARK - ALL-RED REPLACEMENT SHORTS

Print only ONE of the two 3MF projects in this folder, choosing your printer.
Open it as a PROJECT in Bambu Studio so the orientation, supports, and local
connector reinforcement stay loaded. Load red PLA and assign it to the red
part (filament slot 3). There is one printed object and no AMS color changes.

Keep your already printed chest/head and your existing original legs.
The waist key, leg sockets, connector sizes, and sculpture scale are unchanged.
The two old black/gray shorts side inserts are now integrated into these red
shorts. Do not print or glue those old side inserts onto this replacement.
The dark waistband already belongs to the original torso and is unchanged.

PRINT SETUP
- Standard 0.4 mm nozzle; 0.20 mm layers; 2 walls.
- 3% Gyroid in the body; 15% Gyroid and 3 walls locally at all three joints.
- Original 4 top/3 bottom layers retained; tree supports and brim included.
- Print waist-down as arranged. Carefully remove support material from joining
  faces before gluing. The mating geometry has not changed.
- P1S estimate: 9h 57m, 217.9 g.
- P2S estimate: 10h 07m, 217.8 g.
Estimates include supports and preparation; real timing varies with filament
and printer calibration. The enclosed STL is geometry only and does not retain
these settings or the local reinforcement modifiers; prefer the 3MF.

VERIFICATION
Both replacement projects finished slicing in Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 with
successful result codes, empty plate warning messages, and zero color changes.
All generated model/support/brim extrusion paths fit the plate, including the
P1S reserved corner. Actual exported STL reimport is watertight and manifold.
Digital comparisons show zero change to the core containing all connectors.
All three insertion paths pass against the original torso and leg meshes.
The eight other part meshes remain byte-for-byte identical to the originals.
The original user-reported infill error was not reproduced; this replacement
rebuilds the shorts as one closed solid and changes the sparse infill pattern.
These are engine and geometry checks, not a physical test of this replacement.

Original files in KingShark_realistic_47cm_v2 are preserved. Use those for all
