Open King_Shark_Realistic_47cm_P1S_NON_AMS.3mf or the matching P2S project in Bambu Studio.
Choose Open as Project, keep the embedded custom process, and load the plate's stated color.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
11 actual printable glue parts, 6 arranged plates, the editable Blender assembly, and individual STL fallbacks.
Assembled size: 469.54mm tall × 323.27mm wide × 237.93mm deep.
Both soles have a common flat base for stable shelf contact.
Body: black/charcoal; shorts: red with black waistband and sculpted side panels; teeth: white.
Arms and hands stay together; lower legs and feet stay together. There are no unnecessary limb color cuts.
The approved concept guided this mesh. The generated rear anatomy is an interpretation, and photographic skin mottling/pores are not all physical geometry.
Actual geometry previews are in previews/. The source concept is clearly marked separately.

PRINTING
Standard-flow 0.4mm nozzle, PLA, textured PEI, 0.20mm layers.
Two walls, four top layers and three bottom layers. Main parts use 10% lightning; legs use 2% gyroid.
Joint regions locally use three walls and 15% gyroid. The 3MF preserves these modifiers.
Automatic tree supports use 0.20mm separation and 0.35mm XY gap. Keep the saved orientations/brims.
The closed CAD solids are filled lightly by these slicer settings. Do not geometrically hollow them again.
STL files contain geometry only; they do not store supports, infill, colors, local reinforcement or printer settings.

ASSEMBLY
Remove supports and brim from all mating faces before gluing. Match names to previews/exploded_assembly.png.
1. Glue both leg keys into the underside of the shorts.
2. Glue the torso onto the large waist key. The curved seam follows the waistband.
3. Glue each complete arm onto its shoulder key, then attach the dorsal fin.
4. Insert each black shorts side panel inward from its own side and glue into its matching recess.
5. Install the LOWER white tooth arch first: approach from the front/slightly above, slide rearward then down into its seat.
6. Install the UPPER arch second: approach from the front/slightly below, slide rearward then up into its seat.
Do not cut off the hidden gum rails; they keep the teeth connected and locate the arches.
Use a small amount of super glue on hidden contact surfaces and seat the visible faces together.
