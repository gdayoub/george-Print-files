## Suggested title

**Dragon + Pixel Display Shelves | Modular PLA Display Ledges**

## Description draft

Give your small prints a display of their own. Choose a dragon-wing ledge, an arcade-style pixel pod, or a plain ledge. Flat decorative pieces let you use one colour throughout or add contrasting accents without repeated filament swaps.

Designed around the Bambu P1S and a 0.4 mm nozzle, each part fits within its build volume. The cores export on their sides and decorations export flat. Editable source and detailed assembly notes accompany the STL geometry.

**Prototype notice:** geometry passed mesh checks, but physical printing and mounting are not validated. Adhesive attachment to textured painted walls remains unresolved. No working load is assigned. These files are for prototype development, not a verified adhesive shelf installation.

Choose the plain ledge for the fewest parts, add wings and crest for the dragon, or print the pod and front frame for the pixel version. Check object dimensions first: usable deck depth behind the lip is 58.4 mm.

If you enjoy the design, consider a Boost when available, share a make, or leave constructive feedback. Support is optional and appreciated.

## Before a general-use release

- Print the parts and record printer, nozzle, filament, plate, settings, material use and elapsed time.
- Resolve mounting and record physical tests; do not adopt the reference images’ load claims.
- Slice and physically verify any uploaded 3MF profile. Do not label STL geometry as a verified profile.
- Add real print photos and keep renders labelled.
- Choose a model license and verify rights for included assets.
- Add your profile URL to the root `catalog.json` and `docs/SUPPORT.md`. Add the published model URL to its `project.json`, catalog entry and README.
