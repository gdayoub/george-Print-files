## Description

Big shapes, simple printing goals and a little character for the things you make. Emberwing brings a sweeping dragon silhouette to three display tiers. Rift uses one bold hexagonal pod you can repeat in your own arrangement. Both designs target single-colour PLA, with separate parts sized for a Bambu P1S.

This is an **experimental prototype**. The STL meshes are checked, but printing, assembly and adhesive mounting are not physically verified. No load capacity or mounting method for textured painted walls has been validated. Read the included print guide before making a full set.
