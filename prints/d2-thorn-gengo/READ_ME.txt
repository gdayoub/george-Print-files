D2 THORN - SEPARATE GENGO CLICK-IN BADGE

Open D2_Thorn_Gengo_click_in.3mf in Bambu Studio.
The original Downloads/D2_thorn_agent_before.3mf has not been changed.

FOUR PLATES
1. Gold cartridge - filament 2
2. Black handle and fittings - filament 1
3. Green details and Gengo - filament 3
4. Black shell with Gengo socket - filament 1

The badge is its own selectable object: "Gengo - click-in green badge".
It prints flat on the existing green plate, alongside the four green details.
The project therefore stays at four plates. Prime tower is disabled because
every plate uses one filament. Ordinary startup purging still occurs.

FIT TEST FIRST
Open Gengo_fit_test_first.3mf. Print its black socket sample on plate 1 and
green badge on plate 2. The sample uses the same socket shape as the shell.
Both plates together are estimated at about 24 minutes and 3.3 g, including
separate printer startup cycles. The green badge can be used on the final prop.

After cooling, remove any first-layer flare and debris. The two long slots
in the badge must be clear; these let the narrow outer tabs flex inward.
Align the badge with the recess and press evenly on its two ends. The small
side ramps compress the tabs; their shoulders engage the recessed catches.
Do not force a tight badge. Lightly dress the two small catches if needed,
then retry the sample. Leave mating surfaces unpainted until checking fit.
For the final upright shell, clear any support remnants from the recess.
The coupon prints with the recess upward, whereas the shell prints upright;
surface finish and dimensional error may differ between those orientations.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
The badge is one connected, watertight mesh, about 23 x 12.9 x 3.2 mm.
The modified shell and fit sample are also watertight. The assembled badge
has no mesh collision with the socket. Designed entrance clearance is
0.25 mm per side, with approximately 0.20 mm snap-tab deflection.
The physical snap fit has not been print-tested. Use the sample before
committing to the full shell; actual fit depends on filament and calibration.

All four main plates and both test plates were successfully sliced with
the installed Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61, P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16 mm layers.
All revised plates report zero filament changes and no prime tower.
Badge supports and brim are off; it uses 100% infill and slower face printing.
The other existing parts, plate positions, and material assignments are kept.

SPELLING
The source file's text said "Gango". This version uses "Gengo", matching
the written request. The raised text and badge backing print together in green.
