DEVIL FRUIT - RED, BLACK AND WHITE

START HERE
Open Devil_Fruit_P1S_Separate_Plates.3mf in Bambu Studio AS A PROJECT.
This is the corrected native Bambu project with P1S 0.4 mm settings.
It contains four separate plates. Print each plate once:
  Plate 1: Black backing
  Plate 2: Black spiral overlay
  Plate 3: Red fruit
  Plate 4: White curled stem
Select the appropriate filament for each plate. No filament changes during
an individual plate are required. Do not scale or auto-orient the parts.

The STL - Individual Parts folder is an alternative for importing parts
individually. You do not need to import these when using the Bambu project.
Import STLs as millimeters at 100% scale; all are already face-up at Z=0.

SIZE AND PRINT SETTINGS
Finished size: 213.3 mm wide x 240 mm tall x 9.6 mm thick.
Saved settings: 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 4 top and bottom layers,
10% gyroid infill, supports OFF, no brim.
Use your calibrated filament temperature and flow settings.
Your black Creality Hyper PLA and red Soyelin Ultra PLA provide two colors;
use white PLA for the stem.
Estimated total: about 237 g of PLA and 6 hours 49 minutes across four jobs.
Actual time and material consumption may differ.

ASSEMBLY
1. Let all parts cool flat. Remove strings and first-layer flare from
   mating edges. Dry-fit everything before applying glue.
2. Seat the red body in the large recess in the black backing.
3. Test the black overlay over the backing's three square keys. It rests
   on the red body and the backing rim. Do not force the spiral network.
4. Test the white stem in its shaped opening. Its face should sit flush
   with the black outline. Remove the overlay and inserts after testing.
5. Apply a thin layer or small dots of PLA-compatible adhesive under the
   red insert and seat it. Avoid thick glue beads that alter the height.
6. Glue the black overlay to the red body and rim, lowering it over the
   three keys. Keep it level while the adhesive cures.
7. Glue the white stem into its opening, keeping glue off visible edges.

This is an aligned glue assembly, not a snap fit. Pocket clearance is
0.25 mm per side. Keys are 3.8 mm wide; sockets are 4.3 mm wide.
The key ends intentionally stop 0.2 mm below the black face.
The black spirals stand 2.4 mm above the red body.

WALL MOUNTING
The back is completely flat. Place Command strips or suitable mounting
tape on the broad rear fruit body, distributed across upper and lower
areas. Follow the adhesive manufacturer's surface preparation, load,
and removal instructions. No wall hook is needed or supplied.

CHECKS
All four meshes passed digital manifold, watertightness, and size checks.
The native project was reloaded and all four plates sliced successfully
in Bambu Studio with its saved P1S settings, no plate warnings, and no
filament changes. Physical fit and mounting have not been test-printed.
