BATMAN BEYOND — SNAP TOGETHER, NO GLUE

USE THIS KIT, NOT THE EARLIER GLUE-FIT MODEL.
P1S / 0.4 mm nozzle / 0.2 mm layers / Textured PEI Plate
200 mm wide x 240 mm tall x 5.6 mm assembled depth.

FIRST: PRINT THE SMALL FIT TEST
Inside FIT_TEST_FIRST, open and print these two projects:
  01_BLACK_FIT_TEST.3mf — black PLA, approximately 21 minutes.
  02_RED_FIT_TESTS.3mf — red PLA, approximately 16 minutes.
The three black pockets are identical. The red tiles are identified
by square notches along one edge:
  ONE notch = looser socket.
  TWO notches = STANDARD socket (start here).
  THREE notches = firmer socket.
Let everything cool. Flip a red tile so its socket faces the black
catch. Press it straight into any pocket, supporting the base below.
Choose the fit that seats with moderate finger pressure and stays
retained when gently lifted. Do not force a tight tile. If none seats
and holds cleanly, adjust the fit before printing the large red set.
These PLA clips are for initial assembly and occasional removal,
not repeated clicking/fidgeting. Physical performance is untested.

THEN: PRINT THE TWO NUMBERED MAIN FILES
  01_PRINT_BLACK_SNAP_BASE.3mf
    Creality Hyper PLA black: about 2 h 32 min / 92.2 g.
  02_PRINT_RED_SNAP_INSERTS_STANDARD.3mf
    Soyelin Ultra PLA red: about 47 min / 17.8 g.
    All NINE red inserts are on the plate. Print the red job ONCE.
Total main-kit estimate: about 3 h 19 min / 110 g.

If your fit test favors another socket size, use RED_LOOSER_FIT.3mf
or RED_FIRMER_FIT.3mf from ALTERNATIVE_RED_FITS INSTEAD OF the
standard red job. The same black base works with all three choices.

OPEN AND PRINT
Open each 3MF as a project in Bambu Studio. P1S, placement and sliced
toolpaths are included. Confirm your loaded filament/AMS slot and
Textured PEI Plate. Re-slice if you change plate or filament settings.
Profiles use Generic PLA; temperatures/flow are not spool-calibrated.
Keep the saved orientations and 100% scale. No supports or color
changes are required. Do not auto-orient the red pieces.

ASSEMBLY
Black prints rear-down, catches UP. Red prints show-face DOWN with
sockets UP; flip red pieces after removal from the plate.
Align the matching pixel outlines and sockets, then press each catch
location shown in ASSEMBLY_PRESS_LOCATIONS.png. There are twelve
hidden catches across the nine inserts. No glue or extra pins.
Rear surface remains completely flat for mounting tape.

WHAT CHANGED FOR SNAPS
The black base remains 4.8 mm thick. Pockets are now 2.4 mm deep,
with a continuous 2.4 mm black floor. Red parts are 3.2 mm thick,
including a flat 0.8 mm show-face skin, and project 0.8 mm above black.
Hidden square catches have 45-degree ramps. Four flexible 1.2 mm
socket walls bend around each catch. Visible front pixel art is
unchanged. Hidden ramps replace the earlier all-vertical connector
geometry so the parts can lock while printing without supports.
The faster black job uses 20% infill and ordinary INTERNAL infill
bridges. Red inserts use solid infill and no bridge toolpaths.

CHECKED
All component meshes: watertight, manifold and single connected solids.
Assembly: no at-rest collisions; a 0.4 mm rigid withdrawal intersects
each catch, verifying modeled positive retention rather than friction
alone. This geometric check does not measure real snap force or strain.
All six projects successfully sliced, with no support toolpaths and
model extrusion paths inside the P1S bed/exclusion limits.
Print the fit test: no physical snap or durability test has been done.
