900 mm modular wall emblem

35.4 × 12.6 inches. Five sections. Red on black. Concealed snap keys.

Start with 00_PRINT_FIRST_fit_test.3mf. The digital geometry and slicing checks passed; this design has not been physically printed. The coupon tests your P1S, filament and snap fit before the large pieces. All files are already at the intended size.

1. Print and test the small coupon

  1. Open 00_PRINT_FIRST_fit_test.3mf as a project in Bambu Studio. Select your P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle and your actual build plate. Map filament 1 to black Creality Hyper PLA.
  2. The supplied baseline uses Generic PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 100% rectilinear infill, no supports and no brim. Use your calibrated filament temperatures and flow. Keep the XY scale at 100%.
  3. Label the three keys before removing them: left = loose; middle = nominal; right = snug. Let the parts cool. Clear any strings from the socket; do not scrape away its retaining lip.
  4. Put the two coupon halves face down on a flat surface and align their seam with a small 0.2 mm gap. Start with the nominal key. Its flat, bed-facing side remains visible at the back; the tapered side goes into the socket. Press evenly until the shoulders pass the lip and the key sits recessed.
  5. The halves should stay aligned without excessive force, cracking or whitening. If too tight, try loose. If loose, try snug. If none holds properly, stop and adjust the connector fit before printing the full sign. Use a thin plastic pick through a split in the key to remove it gently.

2. Print the five main sections

Open P01–P05_AMS_black_red.3mf one at a time as projects. Each file is a separate plate with the two materials already aligned: filament 1 = black base; filament 2 = red face. Map red to your Soyelin Ultra PLA. Re-slice with your own calibrated PLA profiles and inspect the preview before printing.

Main plate baseline
0.20 mm layers · 3 walls · 5 top + 5 bottom layers · 12% gyroid · no supports · flat rear side on the bed. Black runs from Z 0–6.0 mm; red runs from 6.0–7.2 mm. There is one AMS color change per section. The rear sockets require short bridges; the fit coupon checks that behavior. Prime tower is disabled for this single height-based color change; check the red transition for your filaments and enable a tower if needed, then re-arrange within the bed.

The largest section is about 194 × 208 mm. All five were sliced successfully with the P1S profile, with no slicer warnings. Estimates: 230 g and 6.6 hours for the five main plates; about 5 g and 18 minutes for one key plate. Allow extra material for your flushing settings, test coupon and failed prints.

Print one 06_KEYS plate matching the fit you chose. Each contains eight keys: six for the sign and two spares. Do not print all three key variants unless you want them.

3. Assemble the sign face down

  1. Lay the red faces down on a clean, soft, flat surface. From the rear, the order is P05, P04, P03, P02, P01. Bring the matching edges together; small 0.2 mm seams are intentional.
  2. Install the six tested keys from the back. The two end joins take one key each; the two central joins take two keys each. Align the slanted end keys with their sockets. Press the panels flat while seating the keys.
  3. Check that every key is recessed below the flat rear surface. Lift and handle the assembled sign with both hands, supporting the outer tips. These PLA keys are intended for occasional assembly, not repeated flexing.

4. Mount with removable strips

The cyan areas show where a 24 × 64 mm adhesive body fits on each flat back. They are available surface areas, not a certified Command mounting pattern. Check the dimensions of your actual strip, any removal-tab clearance, and the permitted orientation. Use suitable removable picture-hanging strips and support each section; keep adhesive away from seams and keys.

Test adhesion on the printed PLA and your actual wall before hanging the full sign. A textured print surface may need smoothing in the adhesive area. Follow the strip maker's surface preparation, wall compatibility, wait time and removal instructions. The strip rating does not verify adhesion to 3D-printed PLA. Weigh your finished print when selecting strips.

3M specifies clean, smooth compatible surfaces; its picture-hanging instructions include a one-hour wait and at least seven days after painting. See Command's application instructions and surface guidance.

STL fallback: the COMBINED STL for each panel is a single solid. Add a layer color change after 6.0 mm of black, before the first red layer at Z 6.2 mm with 0.20 mm layers. Alternatively import a panel's BLACK and RED STLs together as parts of one object, preserving their relative Z positions. The separate red STL is not intended as a separately printed glue-on insert.

Blender units: the scene is physically 0.900 m wide. 3MF and STL exports are in millimeters. Do not scale the whole sign to fit one bed, and do not resize the panels individually. Source code and validation reports are in Source/.

What was verified
20 STL meshes are watertight with outward winding. Each plate fits the P1S bed with margin. All 18 seated key/variant combinations have zero modeled collision volume. All nine Bambu Studio projects sliced successfully. The original Higgsfield scene contains 16 closed meshes. Physical snap retention, repeated use and wall adhesion have not been tested.
