GENGAR V6 - ONLY TWO PRINT FILES
Bambu P1S / 0.4 mm nozzle / Textured PEI plate / 100% scale

1. Open 01_BLACK_BODY_P1S.3mf in Bambu Studio.
   Choose your BLACK Creality Hyper PLA AMS slot.
   About 79 minutes, 44.1 g including supports.

2. Open 02_RED_DETAILS_P1S.3mf.
   Choose your RED Soyelin Ultra PLA AMS slot.
   About 13 minutes, 2.3 g. All five face pieces are on one plate.

Combined slicer estimate: 92 minutes, 46.4 g.
Two single-color jobs avoid repeated AMS color changes.
Handling, assembly and optional extra calibration are additional.

Remove the external tree supports. Try your cool/off Core in the body first,
with its button toward the rear center between the ears. Check button access.
The opening is 43.8 mm, matching your tested ring. The full holder and retention
have not been physically tested; don't force it. Lift out for charging/cleaning.

Dry-fit the red pieces, then glue them into the matching recesses with a tiny
amount of PLA-compatible glue on their backs. Keep glue out of the device cavity.
These are seated/glued inserts, not verified snap-fit parts.

The projects use stock PLA starting profiles, 0.20 mm layers and 3 walls.
If your plate differs from Textured PEI, select the correct plate and reslice.
