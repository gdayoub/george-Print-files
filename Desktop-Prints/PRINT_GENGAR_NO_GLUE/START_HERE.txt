GENGAR - NO GLUE VERSION
Bambu P1S / 0.4 mm nozzle / Textured PEI plate / 100% scale

1. Print 01_BLACK_BODY_P1S.3mf in BLACK Creality Hyper PLA.
   About 79 minutes, 44.1 g. It is exactly the same body as V6.
   If you already printed the V6 body, reuse it.

2. Print 02_RED_PRESS_FIT_P1S.3mf in RED Soyelin Ultra PLA.
   About 13 minutes, 2.3 g. All five updated face pieces are on one plate.
   These replace the previous glue-in red pieces.

Choose the matching AMS slot for each job. Total estimate: about 1 hour
32 minutes and 46.4 g, excluding handling and optional extra calibration.

ASSEMBLY
Let the parts cool. Remove external supports, including any remnants inside
the face recesses. With the Core removed, align each red piece with its matching
recess and press it straight inward with even thumb pressure. Small tapered
ribs are designed to grip the recess walls. No glue, screws or magnets are used.
Keep the tiny ribs; they are intentional. Do not hammer or force an insert.
If a piece will not seat or stays loose, its fit needs adjustment.

The no-glue attachment is designed and slicer-checked, but not physically tested.
The 43.8 mm Core opening is unchanged. Only the fit ring was tested by you;
full-holder insertion, button reach and retention remain untested.
Try the cool/off Core with its button toward the rear center between the ears.
Lift the device out for charging or cleaning.

Both jobs use 0.20 mm layers and stock PLA starting profiles. If your installed
plate differs, select the correct plate in Bambu Studio and reslice.
