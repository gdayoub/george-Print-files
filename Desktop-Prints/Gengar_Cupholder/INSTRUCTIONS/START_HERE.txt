GENGAR FAST PRINT - SEPARATE COLOR INSERTS

USE THESE THREE PROJECTS, ONE AT A TIME:
01_BODY_Purple_P1S.3mf - purple body with matching face recesses.
02_EYES_Red_P1S.3mf - both red eye inserts, already laid flat.
03_TEETH_White_P1S.3mf - one white grin insert, already laid flat.

Open each as its own project in Bambu Studio and slice separately. Do not combine the three colors on a single plate for print-by-layer. Each project uses only one filament, and prime towers are disabled. An AMS is not required: you can change filament between the three print jobs.

P1S STARTING SETTINGS
Designed for the stock 0.4 mm nozzle. Body: 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 12% gyroid, tree auto supports. Eyes/grin: 0.16 mm layers, 3 walls, 12% gyroid, supports disabled. Generic PLA and textured PEI are starting profiles; select your actual filament, plate and nozzle. Print every file at 100% scale in millimeters. Review the body supports beneath arms and overhangs before printing. STL copies are included as a fallback; these do not contain printer settings.

ASSEMBLY
1. Let the prints cool and remove any supports or brim. Keep the insert backs flat.
2. Dry-fit the eyes and grin into the matching front recesses. The eye shapes are mirrored. Side clearance is 0.25 mm per side, with a 0.25 mm space behind the inserts. These are glue-in pieces, not snap fits. Lightly sand a tight edge if your printer's fit requires it.
3. Apply a small amount of plastic-compatible glue to the recesses and seat each piece with its sculpted face outward. Follow the glue's curing instructions.
4. Use a fine black acrylic paint pen for the pupil slits, tooth separation grooves and outlines. The preview shows the printed pieces before adding black paint. White teeth print as a single connected grin, so there are only three inserts to attach.

The earlier small finger recesses and tall Gengar body are retained. The deep can pocket remains 69.2 mm wide and 112 mm deep. Overall dimensions are approximately 142.64 x 119.82 x 144.97 mm.

VALIDATION
Body and all inserts are watertight solids. Re-imports of all STL and 3MF files passed. Insert/body collision checks passed with designed fitting clearance. A 66.2 mm diameter can has a clear vertical insertion path. Actual print time must come from your slicer. Fit and strength have not been physically test-printed.
