ASSEMBLY
Let parts cool and remove external supports, including remnants in face recesses.
With the Core removed, align each red insert and press it straight into its
matching recess using even thumb pressure. Keep the tiny grip ribs. No glue,
screws or magnets are specified. Do not hammer or force an insert; if it will
not seat or stays loose, its fit needs adjustment.

Try the cool/off intact Core cup in the holder, button toward the rear center
between the ears. Check full seating and button reach. Lift out for charging
and cleaning. Printed parts do not replace the Core cup or vapor path.

The cavity dimensions now come from your KoffingCore_colored.3mf, whose creator
reports Proxy V1 and V2 compatibility. The Gengar holds the cup upright.
This specific Gengar's full fit, retention and red attachment remain physically
untested. The previously tested 43.8 mm ring is supporting evidence only.

Both jobs use 0.20 mm layers and stock PLA starting profiles. If your plate
differs from Textured PEI, select the correct plate in Bambu Studio and reslice.
