# Enjoy the print? Help shape the next one.

[← Back to the gallery](../README.md)

Thanks for checking out my designs. Seeing something I worked on become part of someone else’s setup is the best part of sharing these files.

## A few ways to help

- **Star the repository** so you can find the collection again.
- **Share a make** with a photo and your printing settings. A real-world result helps others decide what to print.
- **Report a rough edge**: a tight fit, confusing instruction, or better orientation is useful feedback.
- **Suggest the next design** through a [design idea](https://github.com/gdayoub/george-Print-files/issues/new?template=design-idea.yml).
- **Consider a MakerWorld Boost** when a model is published, if you like it and have a Boost available.

## MakerWorld — coming soon

My verified profile and model links will appear here and on each project page after publication. There is no live Boost link for these shelves yet.

Once a listing is available, open its MakerWorld page and use the Boost option offered there. Availability and eligibility follow the platform’s current rules. Support is optional and is never required to access the files offered here. There are no promised rewards or Boost exchanges.

[About MakerWorld’s Boost system](https://blog.bambulab.com/boosting-what-matters-rewarding-engineering-and-design-effort/)

[Share a make or printing issue](https://github.com/gdayoub/george-Print-files/issues/new?template=print-report.yml). Clear, respectful feedback is welcome whether a print worked perfectly or taught you something the hard way.
